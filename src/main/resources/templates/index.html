The process

How It Works

A simplified four-step process designed to minimize waiting times and maximize security.

1

Apply Online

Fill out the required forms and upload supporting documents securely through our portal.

2

Book Appointment

Schedule a convenient time at your nearest service center for verification.

3

Biometric Capture

Visit the center to provide secure fingerprints and an official photograph.

4

Secure Delivery

Receive your official documents via tracked, secure courier delivery.
