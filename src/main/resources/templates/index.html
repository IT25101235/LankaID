How it works

A clear four-step process

Complete the online steps before attending any required in-person verification.

  1. 1

    Apply Online

    Complete the relevant form and submit the required supporting documents.

    Online
  2. 2

    Book Appointment

    Choose an available time at the most convenient service centre.

    Online
  3. 3

    Verify Identity

    Attend the centre for document checks and biometric capture where required.

    At a service centre
  4. 4

    Receive Document

    Track the final status and receive the completed identity document securely.

Support and assistance

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to visit a service centre?

Some services need in-person document checks and biometric capture. You complete the online steps first, then book a time at a centre.

How do I follow my application?

Sign in to your LankaID account to see each stage of your application.

I lost my walk-in QR pass

Enter the mobile number you used for booking and request your QR code again.

Which documents can I apply for?

National Identity Cards and passports: new applications, renewals and replacements for lost or damaged documents.
